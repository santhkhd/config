Privacy Policy

Malayalam Movie List ("the App") is provided as a free, ad-supported application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We prioritize user privacy. The App does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, precise location, camera recordings, or microphone audio.

Permissions Transparency:
  • Internet & Network State: To fetch curated Malayalam movie information, public news RSS feeds, online radio streams, and video metadata.
  • Foreground Service (Media Playback): To allow uninterrupted background playback of Malayalam live radio stations with notification media controls.
  • Push Notifications: To deliver occasional updates regarding new movie releases, trailer drops, or important announcements (user opt-in).

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs to provide core services, advertising, and crash reporting. These third parties may collect anonymous identifiers, device data, and usage metrics:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Firebase Analytics & Cloud Messaging
  • OneSignal (Push Notifications)

3. Embedded Media and External Content

The App features public media links, official movie trailers, YouTube channel feeds, and links to external news providers:

  • YouTube Videos: Videos and trailers are embedded and played using standard YouTube IFrame embeds adhering strictly to the YouTube Terms of Service. We do not download videos, bypass advertisements, or play video audio while the screen is turned off.
  • External Web Links & E-Papers: When you open web articles or OTT streaming providers, content is delivered directly from the respective publisher's website. We recommend reviewing their independent privacy policies.

4. Data Retention and Security

Any user preferences (such as bookmarked favorites or custom category selections) are stored locally on your device using Room Database / SharedPreferences and are never transmitted to external servers. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect user data.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service is general audience and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

Effective Date: September 2026

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the developer contact page on the Google Play Store.
