Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Zimbabwe Newspapers ("we", "our", or "the app") provides users convenient access to publicly available news headlines, web portals, live video feeds, and public radio streams from Zimbabwe and across the globe.

1. Information We Collect

This app does not require account registration or the collection of personally identifiable information (PII). We may use industry-standard SDKs such as Google Play Services and AdMob to deliver relevant advertisements and measure general app stability and analytics.

2. Third-Party Content & Web Links

Our app aggregates links and feeds to external websites and official publishers (including The Herald, Daily News, NewsDay, The Chronicle, and others). When you browse external newspaper websites, their respective privacy policies and terms apply.
