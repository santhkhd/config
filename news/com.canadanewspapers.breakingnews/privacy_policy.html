Privacy Policy

Canada News: Local & Weather ("the App") is provided as a free, ad-supported application. This service is intended for use as is.

Information Collection and Use

The App aggregates publicly available RSS feeds, news websites, online radio stations, and public YouTube video broadcasts. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal identifiable information directly on our servers.

Third Party Services & Advertising

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase & Cloud Messaging
  • Open-Meteo Weather API

Content Ownership & Attribution

All news articles, trademarks, channel logos, RSS feeds, and newspaper content displayed within the App belong to their respective publishers, broadcasters, and media organizations. Canada News acts solely as a news aggregator and reader.
