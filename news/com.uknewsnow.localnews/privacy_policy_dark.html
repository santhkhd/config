Privacy Policy & Content Disclaimers

USA Newspapers & News ("the App") is provided as a free, ad-supported news aggregator. This document details our privacy practices, data safety commitments, Advertising ID usage, and news aggregation disclaimer.

1. News Aggregator & Content Disclaimer

IMPORTANT NOTICE

USA Newspapers & News is a news and multimedia aggregator. We index and provide convenient access to publicly available RSS feeds, YouTube video channels, live news streams, and official newspaper websites.

  • Copyright & Ownership: All article titles, excerpts, videos, channel names, avatars, logos, and trademarks belong exclusively to their respective copyright holders, broadcasters, and news publishers.
  • Non-Affiliation: The App is an independent directory and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected with any government entity, newspaper, broadcaster, or YouTube channel featured herein.
  • Content Inquiries & DMCA Takedown: If you are a copyright owner or publisher and wish to update or remove your feed, channel, or listing from the App, please contact us via our Google Play developer support email for prompt removal.

2. Information Collection and Permissions

We value your privacy. The App does not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, financial info, or precise GPS location.

Permissions Transparency:
  • Internet & Network State: Required to fetch news feeds, headlines, video streams, and online radio broadcasts.
  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used strictly by Google AdMob SDK (via com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) to serve non-personalized and personalized advertisements and for fraud prevention.
  • Foreground Service (Media Playback): Enables uninterrupted audio playback for live radio and audio news when minimized, with media notification controls.
  • Push Notifications (Optional): Sends occasional breaking headlines (requires Android 13+ user consent).

3. Advertising ID (AD_ID) & Google Play Data Safety

In compliance with Google Play's Developer Policy on Advertising IDs:

  • The App requests the Google Advertising ID solely for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).
  • We do not link the Advertising ID to any personally identifiable information.
  • User Control: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may process anonymous device identifiers and operational metrics:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Firebase Analytics & Cloud Messaging
  • OneSignal (Push Notifications)

5. Embedded Media & YouTube Terms of Service

Videos within the app are delivered using standard YouTube player integrations adhering to the YouTube Terms of Service. We do not download or store video files, bypass advertisements, or allow background video audio when the screen is locked.

6. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between the App and external servers is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS protocol. User favorites, reading history, and app preferences are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes and Updates

We may periodically update this policy to reflect app features and policy updates.
